A one-page infographic summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026: the COP31 climate talks ending without a deal in Antalya, suspected Ukrainian sea drones striking a Russian warship at Benghazi, Iran's strikes on a Bahrain industrial site and tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, new UK–EU post-Brexit trade and travel deals, Mexico's capture of cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro", and a fatal boat capsizing in Nigeria. The image is drawn from scratch with PIL (`draw.py`, shapes and DejaVu text only, no photos), and `main.ts` copies it to the output path.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
